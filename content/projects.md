Things I've built and still (mostly) maintain.

### [chess-results](https://github.com/markjonleonard/chess-results)

Read Swiss tournaments from chess-results.com: pairings, colours, floats and the byes the site deletes, with FIDE TRF(x) export for pairing engines.

### [ecf-rating](https://github.com/markjonleonard/ecf-rating)

Read player ratings, clubs and games from the English Chess Federation rating database.

### [trowbridgechess](https://github.com/markjonleonard/trowbridgechess)

The [Trowbridge Chess Club](https://www.trowbridgechessclub.co.uk) website and its supporting applications.
