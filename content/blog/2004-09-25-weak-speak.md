I expect that you're wondering what the term **weak speak** means. So am I. It just popped out of my mouth one day before my brain had time to analyse it. I think I meant it as a loose pun on l33tspeak. There are precedents for prefixing the word speak in this way (thank you Mr. Orwell). Perhaps it's a meme waiting to happen. It does have a pleasing rhyme, after all.

A quick google confirmed that my coinage isn't original, but no-one had claimed it for their own. I registered the domain name on a whim about ten minutes later. For various reasons I wish hadn't[^1] bothered now, but I might as well make use of it. Hence this blog - I'm too lazy to actually create a proper site. What would it contain, anyway?

[^1]: I wish *I* hadn't. A fitting start for a blog about lazy writing. (Footnote added Sep 2026.)
