This is me linking to a "[bitch](http://www.bbc.co.uk/ouch/opinion/b1tch/db_vs_tv_viewers.shtml)" (on the BBC website) about the media discussing newspaper articles reporting that some parents thought their children might be scared by a one-armed presenter on a Children's television programme.

Although it turns out the BBC had received only nine formal complaints. Isn't this Brand and Ross all over again? What a circular, reflexive, memetic world we live in.

And the "funny" thing is that I'm most curious to know why they used **b1tch** in the URL instead of simply the word **bitch**.
