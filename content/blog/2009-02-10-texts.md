What's the plural of text (as in [SMS](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_message_service) via mobile phone)? Texts? SMSes? SMSs? Here in the West Country it's pronounced "texis" (think taxis) or even textes (to rhyme with testes). I find this bewildering, bizarre, illogical and improper.
