[This](http://www.thingsmygirlfriendandihavearguedabout.com/), quite literally, is the funniest thing I've read.[^1] I kid you not. It recalls [Jerome K. Jerome](https://www.jeromekjerome.com/) (see [Hampton Court Maze](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/308/pg308-images.html#:~:text=ever%20been%20in%20the%20maze%20at%20Hampton%20Court)) and [Stephen Leacock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Leacock)[^2]. If only (only!) I could write so well.

[^1]: Still online in 2026, in its original 2000s layout: one very long page (about 30,000 words). It's http-only, so your browser may warn you before loading it. Once it's open, your browser's reading mode makes it far easier going: right-click → *Open in reading mode* in Chrome, or Reader (⇧⌘R) in Safari. (Footnote added Sep 2026.)

[^2]: For a taste of Leacock, try ["The Conjurer's Revenge"](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/6340/pg6340-images.html#:~:text=having%20shown%20you%20that%20the%20cloth%20is%20absolutely%20empty) from *Literary Lapses* (1910): a conjurer, heckled all evening by a know-all in the audience, borrows the man's watch, spectacles and hat for one final trick. Exquisite politeness, real hostility, and a hat that will never be the same. (Footnote added Sep 2026.)
