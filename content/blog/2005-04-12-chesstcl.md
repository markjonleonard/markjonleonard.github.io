I'm going to write a chess engine in [Tcl](https://wiki.tcl-lang.org/299). I've not decided on the details but I have chosen the name. It will most likely be a teaching aid. A number of sites purport to provide primers on chess programming, but the C code is often poorly laid out. Tcl script is likely to be more legible.
