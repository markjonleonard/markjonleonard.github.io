The illogical phrase ["filled into"](https://www.google.co.uk/search?hl=en&q=%22filled+into%22) is disconcertingly prevalent as "packaging technology" marketing speak. In this peculiar language products are *filled into*  containers; containers are not usually filled with products. Presumably the latter construction is avoided because it implies that the container becomes full.
