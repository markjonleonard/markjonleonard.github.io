Here's a contraction I've not seen before:

> [kind’ve](https://www.google.com/search?hl=en&q=%22kind%27ve%22)

Yet Google finds 70,000 results![^1] This is the sort of thing up with which I will not put.

[^1]: Google no longer shows result counts up front, and they were only ever rough estimates. [Google Books Ngrams](https://books.google.com/ngrams/graph?content=kind%27ve&year_start=1900&year_end=2022&corpus=en&smoothing=3) is more telling: in published books *kind've* turns up about once per 55,000 *kind of*s, at a steady rate since 2009, and it goes back to at least 1930. So it's rare, stable and older than I thought. (Footnote added Sep 2026.)
